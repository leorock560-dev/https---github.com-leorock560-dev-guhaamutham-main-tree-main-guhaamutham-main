OUR STORY

Rooted in Tradition. Made for Today.

GuhaAmutham LLP is a Coimbatore-based brand of traditional edible oils. We focus on familiar ingredients and the everyday cooking of Indian homes.

Our three oils — coconut, groundnut and gingelly — reflect the ingredients that have long been part of Indian kitchens.
